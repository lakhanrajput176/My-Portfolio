Conversational AI for Credit Card Closure — HSBC (Banking)

Protiviti India | Digital Product Manager, Deputy Manager | Nov 2025 – Present

Context

HSBC's conversational banking platform spans India, Hong Kong, and Singapore. I own the Credit Card Closure chatbot — a Singapore-market use case — end-to-end, alongside broader product ownership of the conversational AI platform across all three markets.

My Role & Key Decisions

Defined the product vision and roadmap for the Credit Card Account Closure flow, translating business requirements into conversational flows and technical user stories in partnership with AI/ML engineers. Coordinated 12–20 stakeholders across engineering, architecture, delivery, QA, conversation design, SRE/platform, and Security/Privacy/Risk/Compliance/Accessibility — prioritizing which flows to automate first based on volume, compliance risk, and containment upside rather than ease of build.   • ✅ Credit Card Account Closure (Singapore): enabled customers to complete the full closure request within the chatbot instead of escalating to a live agent, saving an estimated $120K annually in agent handling cost
  • ✅ Platform-wide: bot containment rose from 68% to 82%, NPS improved by 11 points
  • ✅ Self-service adoption grew 27%, live-agent transfers dropped 24%
  • ✅ Projected $735K+ in 2027 operational savings across the three markets from new use cases

🏥 Healthcare HRMS + AI

Healthcare Workforce Platform + AI Employee Self-Service Chatbot

CloudsR Technology | Senior Product Manager | Jan 2020 – Nov 2025

Context

Hospital clients running this HRMS platform needed to manage complex, healthcare-specific workforce operations — vendor-sourced staffing, credentialing, compliance-heavy leave and attendance tracking, and 24/7 shift scheduling — at a scale of 52,300+ users, while HR teams were buried in manual, repetitive employee queries.

My Role & Key Decisions

Owned the end-to-end roadmap and backlog across employee lifecycle, self-service, and operations modules, prioritizing compliance-critical workflows (background-check clearance, healthcare labor-law leave rules, credentialing) ahead of nice-to-have features given the regulatory stakes of hospital operations. Layered an AI-powered self-service chatbot onto the platform to absorb high-volume, low-complexity queries (leave requests, payslip questions, onboarding FAQs), with escalation logic to route anything ambiguous to a human — a deliberate scope decision to protect trust in a benefits-sensitive HR context rather than chase full automation.

Outcome

  • ✅ The chatbot automated 45% of employee queries and cut response time 50%
  • ✅ The broader platform sustained 95%+ sprint success
  • ✅ 20% lift in customer satisfaction across concurrent product lines

CloudsR Technology | Senior Product Manager | Jan 2020 – Nov 2025
